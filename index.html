Meet the Founder

LEELEE

I’ve always believed there is something inside every woman that the world needs. Sometimes all she needs is someone to walk beside her long enough to remember it.

Go Inspire Now was born from that belief — that every woman, no matter where she is in her journey, deserves a community that sees her, values her, and walks with her toward the woman she was created to be.

— LeeLee, Founder of Go Inspire Now
