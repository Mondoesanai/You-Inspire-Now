THE GO INSPIRE NOW
JOURNEY 25K

More Than a Walk. A Journey Back to Yourself.

Join hundreds of women for an unforgettable day of movement, meaningful conversations, encouragement, celebration, and connection as we walk 25 kilometers together across beautiful Denton, Texas. Whether you’re walking beside lifelong friends or meeting someone new, every step is a reminder that you were never meant to walk alone.

What Makes This Walk Different
  • 25-kilometer guided community walk — over 25,000 steps of purpose
  • Maintained at approximately a 3.0+ mph pace
  • Encouraging pace leaders throughout the route
  • Inspiring mile markers featuring powerful women’s stories
  • Water and refreshment stations along the course
  • Music, encouragement, and cheering squads
  • Opportunities for meaningful conversations while walking
  • Finish-line celebration with music, food trucks, and community
  • Professional photos to capture your journey
  • A welcoming experience for women of all backgrounds
  • Free with registration
Is This Walk for Me?

This is an inspirational community walk — not a race. Participants should be comfortable maintaining approximately a 3.0 mph or faster pace for the duration of the event. We’ll have designated pace leaders, scheduled hydration stops, and an encouraging atmosphere from start to finish.

Our goal isn’t to finish first. Our goal is to finish together.

What to Expect

Every Go Inspire Walk is designed to feel like a breath of fresh air for your soul.

💕

A welcoming community where you belong exactly as you are—no pretending, no performing, no prerequisites.

🚶

Walking routes for all fitness levels. Whether you stroll or stride, every pace is welcome on a Go Inspire Walk.

💬

Genuine conversations with women who get it—real talk, real stories, and real connection that goes deeper than small talk.

✨

New friendships with women who are walking the same journey and ready to walk it beside you.

🌞

Encouragement for whatever season you’re in—whether you’re thriving, rebuilding, or just trying to take the next step.

📷

Beautiful moments worth capturing—and inspiration that stays with you long after the walk ends.

More Than a Walk.

Every Go Inspire Walk is an opportunity to slow down, breathe deeply, reconnect with yourself, and build meaningful relationships with women who simply understand.

Some women come looking for exercise.

Many leave with lifelong friendships.

Every woman leaves reminded that she is seen, valued, and loved.
